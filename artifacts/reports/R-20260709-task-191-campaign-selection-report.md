## Selection

Selected domain: Energy, electricity access, and emissions transition.

Selected capability: Energy transition, access, and emissions-intensity monitoring.

Capability maturity gap: Energy coverage was established but incomplete: MacroForge had bounded energy-use, coal-electricity, energy-balance, and commodity-price evidence without a broad country-year panel for electricity access, clean cooking access, renewable energy, electricity generation/losses, fossil generation mix, energy intensity, and net energy import dependence.

Confidence cell: WDI public API v2 annual scalar country-indicator observations.

Largest compatible campaign: 41 candidates, 22 included, 19 localized zero-row exclusions, 217 countries, 1990-2024.

## Completion-preference rationale

Energy was selected because it is already an established MacroForge domain and the remaining WDI annual-scalar gap could be closed substantially in one large compatible campaign. This better satisfies TASK-191's domain-completion preference than opening another early WDI domain.
