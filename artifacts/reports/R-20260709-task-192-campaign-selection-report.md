# TASK-192 Campaign Selection Report

Status: complete
Date: 2026-07-09

## Selection

Selected domain: Monetary, banking, financial intermediation, and capital-market structure.

Selected analytical capability: Financial-system depth, access, efficiency, stability, and market-structure monitoring.

Capability gap: MacroForge had broad WDI macro-financial and external-vulnerability coverage, but the financial-system domain lacked a broad GFDD-style country-year panel for financial inclusion, institutional depth, market depth, bank efficiency, bank stability, ownership/concentration, and market structure.

Confidence cell: WDI/GFDD public API v2 annual scalar country-indicator observations.

Campaign: WDI/GFDD financial-system depth/access/stability annual-scalar expansion.

## Why this campaign

Selected because the monetary/banking/external-vulnerability domain is already substantially developed and this large GFDD-compatible campaign moves it toward operational completeness without changing architecture.

The domain-completion preference was applied: monetary, banking, financial intermediation, and external-vulnerability evidence was already substantially developed, but GFDD-style financial-system structure was still incomplete. This campaign therefore advanced an already-developed domain rather than opening another early domain.

Provider selection followed capability selection. WDI/GFDD was selected only after identifying the financial-system depth/access/stability confidence cell.
